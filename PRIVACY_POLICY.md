# Privacy Policy for Lucida Downloader Bot

**Effective Date:** September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Lucida Downloader Telegram Bot collects, uses, and protects your information.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Telegram User ID:** We temporarily store your Telegram User ID and username to verify authorization, manage access, and process your download requests.
- **Cookies & Proxy Data:** If you use the `/setcfcookie` or `/setproxy` commands, the bot stores your provided Cloudflare cookies and proxy details in a local, encrypted database solely for the purpose of bypassing Cloudflare restrictions on your behalf during downloads.
- **Message Content:** The bot only processes URLs and commands explicitly sent to it. We do not read or store your personal chat history.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
Your data is used strictly to provide the music downloading service:
- To queue and fulfill your audio download requests.
- To route traffic through your designated proxy to avoid IP bans.

## 3. Data Storage & Security
- All sensitive data (cookies, proxies) is stored in a local SQLite database on the host server.
- We do not share, sell, or distribute your data to any third parties.
- Messages containing sensitive tokens are automatically deleted from the chat upon successful registration to protect your privacy.

## 4. Contact
If you have any questions or wish to have your data permanently deleted from the bot's database, please contact the bot administrator.
